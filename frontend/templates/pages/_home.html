Create playlists from your Navidrome library

Generate, refresh and manage playlists locally. Fast, private, and under your control.

Quick Start

  1. Choose a tool: This Is, Re-Discover or Genre Mix
  2. Configure your playlist: Set the track count and how often it should auto-refresh (daily, weekly, monthly or never).
  3. Let it run: Magic Lists works in the background to keep your playlists fresh.

What it does

Magic Lists reads your local library files and uses their metadata to curate huge lists according to your listening patterns. These huge lists are then given to a local or API AI model for fine selection, those fine grained selections are then composed into a playlist stored in your Navidrome Instance.

Key features

This Is

A definitive, single-artist mix. Includes primary and featured appearances, de-duplicates repeated versions, and balances hits and deep cuts.

Re-Discover

Surfaces tracks you haven't played recently so you can re-find forgotten favourites. Set it to refresh daily, weekly or monthly.

Genre Mix

A curated mix of specific genres selected by a weighted percentage of relevant and forgotten tracks for an AI‑driven curation, finely tuned by the chosen filters.

Auto-Refresh

Schedule playlists to refresh automatically on your machine so lists stay current without manual effort.

Privacy & control

Your music data remains yours. No music files are uploaded, only metadata like title - album - artist are used for curation — you choose whether AI runs locally or via a remote API key.

About this project

MagicLists is an open-source experiment in AI-assisted music discovery for people who run their own Navidrome server. Instead of "renting" playlists from Apple or Spotify, you keep full control of your library and still enjoy smart, personalised mixes.

This is an ongoing research project founded by Ricky Synnot with 20+ years of experience in tech, built with and for the Navidrome community. Now this project's fork is improved and maintained by Miles Pickull, who has over 15 years of experience in tech, we're proving that AI‑powered curation can be free, transparent, and privacy‑respecting.
