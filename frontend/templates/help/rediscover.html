Re-Discover

Re-Discover builds a playlist from tracks you have played before but not recently. You choose the playlist length and refresh frequency, then generate the playlist from your listening history.

Create a playlist in 2 steps
  1. 1 Choose the playlist length. This sets the target size of the final playlist after curation.
  2. 2 Choose the refresh frequency. This controls whether the playlist stays static or rebuilds on a schedule.
What each option changes

Playlist length

Sets the target size of the final playlist after curation. A larger playlist size will result in a possibly larger curation pool, which results in a larger AI request and more reasoning.

Refresh frequency

Controls whether the playlist stays static or rebuilds on a schedule. Rebuilding uses the listening history accumulated since the last run, so a more frequent refresh can surface different forgotten tracks.

Tip: start with the playlist length first. That usually has the biggest impact on the final result.
