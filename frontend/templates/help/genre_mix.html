Genre Mix

Genre Mix builds a curated playlist from one or more genres in your library. You choose the genres, refine the candidate pool with filters, and then shape the final playlist with length and diversity settings.

Create a playlist in 3 steps
  1. 1 Choose your genres. Select one or more genres from your library. A single genre creates a focused deep-dive, while multiple genres blend into a broader mix.
  2. 2 Refine the candidate pool. Use the year range, excluded artists, and minimum quality filters to control which tracks are eligible before curation.
  3. 3 Set the final playlist shape. Choose the playlist length, optional diversity limits, and refresh frequency before creating the playlist.
What each option changes

Choose a genre

Sets the pool of tracks that can appear in the playlist. Selecting more genres makes the result broader and more varied.

Year range

Limits the eras in the candidate pool. A narrower range makes the playlist more era-specific.

Exclude artists

Removes selected artists from the candidate pool. This is useful when you want to avoid over-representing a specific artist or sound.

Minimum quality

Filters out lower-bitrate or lower-bit-depth files before curation. This can reduce the candidate pool when only lower-quality tracks are available, which may result in a smaller or less varied playlist.

Playlist length

Sets the target size of the final playlist after curation.

Diversity limits

Controls how many tracks can come from the same album or artist. Lower values make the playlist more varied, but can reduce the candidate pool when only a few albums or artists are available.

Refresh frequency

Controls whether the playlist stays static or rebuilds on a schedule.

Tip: start with the genre selection and year range first. Those usually have the biggest impact on the overall style of the playlist.
