This Is Artist

This Is Artist builds a playlist focused on a single artist from your library. You choose the artist, decide how many top tracks to include, and then set the final playlist length and refresh behavior.

Create a playlist in 3 steps
  1. 1 Choose an artist. This is the artist the playlist will focus on.
  2. 2 Set the top tracks count. This controls how many popular tracks are fetched for the artist from Navidrome or Last.fm API. Set it to 0 to disable top-track fetching.
  3. 3 Choose the final playlist settings. Set the playlist length and refresh frequency before creating the playlist.
What each option changes

Choose an artist

Sets the focus of the playlist. The final playlist will be built around this artist and the tracks available in your library.

Top tracks per artist

Adds popular tracks for the selected artist. These tracks are fetched from the configured source and will be included in the AI curation pool.

MusicBrainz ID fallback

Used only when the artist needs a Last.fm lookup or no ID is found for the specified artist in your library. It helps identify the artist correctly and does not change which tracks are selected for the playlist.

Playlist length

Sets the target size of the final playlist after curation.

Refresh frequency

Controls whether the playlist stays static or rebuilds on a schedule.

Tip: start with the artist selection and top tracks count first. Those usually have the biggest impact on the final playlist.
